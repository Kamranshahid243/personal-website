Your blog is not a second documentation site. For a personal brand, each post
should make one claim about how you think under constraints.

## Lead with the decision

Open on the tension: what was at stake, what options existed, what you chose.
Readers decide in the first screen whether you sound like someone they want in
a design review.

## Show the trade-off, not the victory lap

Perfect outcomes read as fiction. Name what you gave up — latency for
correctness, speed for operability — and why that was the right call then.

## Keep tutorials in their place

Step-by-step pieces help SEO. Pair them with a short “why this shape” section
so the article still signals seniority.

## End with a transferable lesson

If the only takeaway is “we used library X”, the post expires with the
changelog. Prefer lessons about boundaries, ownership, and measurement.
