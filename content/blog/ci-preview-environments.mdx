A preview that lies is worse than no preview. Reviewers approve fiction, and
production still surprises you on Friday.

## Same image, different config

Build once. Promote the artifact. Inject environment-specific config at deploy
time so “works in preview” means something about the bits you will ship.

## Time-to-live is a feature

Idle previews should expire. Orphaned namespaces are how side projects eat the
infrastructure budget.

## Seed just enough data

Reviewers need representative records, not a full production dump. Synthetic
fixtures with realistic shapes beat anonymisation theatre.

## Link failures to traces

When a preview smoke test fails, the notification should open the failing span
or log line — not a generic “job failed” toast.
